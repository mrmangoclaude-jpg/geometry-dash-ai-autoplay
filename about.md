# AI AutoPlay for Geometry Dash

**AI AutoPlay** is an autonomous pathfinding solver and macro replay engine built for Geometry Dash 2.2 using the Geode SDK.

## Features

- **Visual Fast-Forward Solver**: Watch the AI explore levels at 10x–50x speed with instant respawn until it discovers the optimal path to 100%.
- **All Gamemodes Supported**: Handles Cube, Ship, Ball, UFO, Wave, Robot, Spider, and Swing Copter with specialized corridor-centering and edge-detection logic.
- **Level Library**: Solved runs are automatically saved to disk by Level ID.
- **1-Click AutoPlay**: Instant replay of solved levels at normal speed with 100% precision.
- **Pause Menu & Keybind Integration**: Open the menu from the Pause Layer or press `G` in-game to toggle the solver on the fly.
